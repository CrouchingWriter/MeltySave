# Music credits and source catalogue

This catalogue credits the 35 tracks used in the maintainer's local music collection. **Audio files are not included in the MeltySave release or this repository.** Use the official creator links to obtain music under each creator's terms, then add your files to the game-root `BGM` folder.

Track-to-source matches were checked against SHA-256-identical files in the supplied collection and the accompanying attribution notes. Original song titles and folder names are retained as user data; artist handles are retained where the notes identify a channel. This list is attribution, not a blanket redistribution license or an endorsement by the artists.

## Distribution notes

- The plugin supports user-supplied music. Free use as background music does not automatically grant permission to redistribute a music collection.
- MusMus describes redistribution restrictions and credit placement in its [official FAQ](https://musmus.main.jp/faq.html). Obtain its audio from the official site.
- PeriTune specifies different rules for older CC BY 4.0 tracks and newer releases in its [terms](https://peritune.com/about/). Other creators retain their own terms.
- No audio processing or license conversion is performed by this catalogue. The `_loop` suffix reflects the supplied filename and is not a claim about an original master or its licensing.
- Keep applicable credits with any content you publish using these tracks.
